🚀 App Demonstration & User Flow (Apple App Review Guideline 2.1)

Neon Space Survivor is a 100% offline-playable indie arcade roguelite space shooter. The game is designed for instant playability with zero user friction:

  • No Account / No Login / No Deletion Flow: The app does NOT require any user registration, passwords, or login credentials. Players launch the app and can immediately start playing offline. Account creation/deletion is not applicable.
  • No User-Generated Content (UGC): The app does not contain chat rooms, public comments, or user-uploaded media.
  • No Paid Subscriptions / IAPs: The game is free-to-play with optional Google AdMob rewarded videos for in-game revives/bonuses. No payment credentials or credit card data are ever handled.
  • Gameplay Controls: Tap 'START BATTLE'. Drag to maneuver your ship; primary and secondary weapons fire automatically. Collect cosmic crystals, defeat enemy waves, choose weapon upgrades, and defeat flagships.

[EN] Privacy Policy (English)

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the mobile application developed by Kaunos Games / Caria Kaunos ("Developer", "we", "our") and distributed on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store as Neon Space Survivor (Bundle ID: com.kaunos.neonspace). Protecting player privacy is our absolute highest commitment.

2. Information Collection and Usage

The Game does not collect, solicit, or store sensitive personally identifiable information (such as legal names, residential addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, financial details, or payment card data).

  • Local Device Storage: Game progress, unlocked space vessels, upgrades, crystal balances, and audio/haptic preferences are saved strictly on your local device storage.
  • Anonymous High Scores: Anonymous callsigns and high scores are synchronized using Google Firebase Firestore linked to an anonymous identifier for global leaderboards.
  • Diagnostics and Analytics: Anonymous crash diagnostics and gameplay balance events are processed via Google Firebase Analytics to ensure crash-free execution.
  • Local Notifications: The Game schedules on-device reminders for daily bonus events strictly locally. No remote push notification server is used.
  • Haptics & Vibration: The device's vibration actuator is triggered locally for combat feedback.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob & Apple ATT)

The Game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to present optional rewarded video ads (e.g. emergency revives, crystal bonuses) and occasional interstitials.

Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) & GDPR Compliance: On iOS devices, the app requests tracking consent via Apple's standard ATT framework. If you decline, you will continue to enjoy the complete game experience with non-personalized ads. We delay SDK measurement until consent is handled (GADDelayAppMeasurementInit: true).

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Age Ratings)

The Game is rated for players aged 9+ on the Apple App Store and 10+ on Google Play. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Data Security and Deletion Rights

All cloud network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.3 protocols. Players can reset all local progress at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app data. To delete any anonymous leaderboard entry, players can contact us at our official support email.
