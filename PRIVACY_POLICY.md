# Privacy Policy for Neon Space Survivor

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Neon Space Survivor** (also distributed on Google Play as **"Neon Space: Uzayda Hayatta Kalma"** in Turkish and **"Neon Space Survivor: Rogue Space"** globally; package name: `com.kaunos.neonspace`), developed by **Caria Kaunos** ("we", "our", or "the Developer"), collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

Your privacy is critically important to us.

---

## 1. Overview & Information Collection
The Game does **not** collect, require, or transmit sensitive personally identifiable information (such as your real name, physical address, phone number, contacts, financial or payment information).

- **Local Progress Storage:** Game progression, pilot level, unlocked starships, quantum overclock upgrades, crystal balances, settings, and achievements are stored directly on your device via HTML5 Local Storage.
- **Global Leaderboards & Achievements (Google Firebase & Play Games Services):**
  To support global leaderboard rankings and 32 Play Games achievements, the game synchronizes an anonymous player ID (`uid`), your player-selected callsign, country flag identifier, and high score through **Google Firebase Cloud Firestore** and **Google Play Games Services**.
- **Analytics & Crash Diagnostics:**
  Anonymous gameplay events, performance metrics, and crash diagnostics are processed via **Google Firebase Analytics** to optimize game balance, frame rates, and application stability.
- **Local Notifications (Daily Cosmic Events):**
  The game schedules once-daily local retention notifications (e.g. meteor storm alerts, cosmic supply crates) strictly on the player's device at 20:00 prime-time. No external push server is utilized, and no personal data or notification logs are transmitted outside the device.
- **Device Features:**
  The game utilizes local device haptic vibration motors for tactical collision and firing feedback.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements (Google AdMob)
The Game utilizes **Google AdMob** to display optional rewarded video ads (such as emergency shields, bonus revival, or double reward multipliers) and occasional interstitial transition ads.

Google AdMob, Google Firebase, and Google Play Games Services may collect and process device technical data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID) and Google Play Games Pilot ID
- **Technical Diagnostics:** IP address (used for approximate country-level geographic routing), device brand/model, OS version, and ad performance metrics

Users in the EEA/UK can manage their GDPR consent preferences at any time via the in-game Settings -> "Ad & Privacy Preferences (GDPR)" button.

Review Google's privacy documentation:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob Policies & Guidelines](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy Compliance)
Neon Space Survivor is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above (AdMob Max Ad Content Rating: Teen / PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone 10+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 4. Data Security & Data Deletion Rights
All network communications with Firebase and Google Play Games Services are secured with industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.

- **Local Data Deletion:** Deleting or uninstalling the Game from your device permanently purges all locally stored save files and preferences.
- **Cloud Data Deletion:** Players can permanently wipe their cloud leaderboard record at any time using the in-game **"LİDERLİK TABLOSU VERİLERİMİ SİL (DELETE CLOUD DATA)"** button located directly inside the in-game Privacy modal. Alternatively, players may email us with their pilot callsign, and all associated cloud records will be permanently expunged within 7 business days.
