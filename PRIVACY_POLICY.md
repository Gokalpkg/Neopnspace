# Privacy Policy for Neon Space Survivor

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Neon Space Survivor** ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information. Your privacy is critically important to us.

---

## 1. Overview & Information Collection
**Neon Space Survivor** does not collect, store, or share sensitive personally identifiable information (such as your real name, home address, phone number, or payment details).
- **Local Progress Storage:** Game progress, accumulated crystals, ship upgrades, and settings are stored on your device via local storage.
- **Global Leaderboards & Achievements (Google Firebase & Play Games Services):** To provide global leaderboards and 32 Play Games achievements, an anonymous generated pilot ID (`uid`), your chosen display callsign, country code, and high scores are synchronized via **Google Firebase Cloud Firestore** and **Google Play Games Services**.
- **Analytics & Diagnostics:** Anonymous gameplay events and crash diagnostics are processed via **Google Firebase Analytics** to improve stability and game balance.
- **Device Features:** The game utilizes device vibration (haptic feedback) locally for gameplay feedback.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements (Google AdMob)
The Game uses **Google AdMob** to display optional rewarded advertisements and interstitial transitions.

Google AdMob, Google Firebase, and Google Play Games Services may collect and process information such as:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID) and Gamer Profile ID
- **Technical & Diagnostics Data:** IP address (approximate location), crash logs, and ad performance metrics

Users in the EEA/UK can manage their GDPR consent preferences at any time via the in-game Settings -> "Ad & Privacy Preferences (GDPR)" button.
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob Policies & Guidelines](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Neon Space Survivor is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above (AdMob Max Ad Content Rating: Teen). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 4. Data Security & Data Deletion Rights
All network communication is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Deleting the Game from your device removes all local progress data. To request deletion of your cloud leaderboard callsign and score record, contact us at the email address below with your pilot callsign, and we will permanently delete your record within 7 business days.
