# Privacy Policy for Neon Space Survivor

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Neon Space Survivor** ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information. Your privacy is critically important to us.

---

## 1. Overview & Information Collection
**Neon Space Survivor** does not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information directly.
- **No Account Required:** You can play the entire game without providing your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details.
- **Local Progress Storage:** All game data, including your highest score, accumulated crystals, ship upgrades, and achievements, is stored strictly on your local device via local storage. This data never leaves your device.
- **Device Features:** The game utilizes device vibration (haptic feedback) locally for immersive gameplay feedback.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements (Google AdMob)
The Game uses **Google AdMob** to display optional rewarded video advertisements (e.g., reviving your ship, claiming free crystals, or unlocking extra lucky space chest loot).

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID)
- **Technical & Diagnostics Data:** IP address, crash logs, and ad performance metrics

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please visit:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google AdMob Policies & Guidelines](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

---

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Neon Space Survivor is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to third parties through our game, please contact us immediately.

---

## 4. Data Security
Because all gameplay data is retained locally on your mobile device, deleting the Game from your device will automatically remove all local data. We do not maintain any external user database.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
